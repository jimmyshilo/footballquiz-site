Support

Need help with The Real Football Quiz? Email jimmyshilo@gmail.com. We read every message.

How to play

  • Solo: answer football questions before you run out of lives. Subscribers play 30-question rounds.
  • Teams: two sides take turns. Each team answers 10 questions.
  • Without a subscription you get one free solo round and one free team round.

Subscriptions

Unlock all 150 questions and unlimited play with a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, sold by Apple.

  • To restore a purchase: open the paywall in the App and tap Restore Purchases, or use the same Apple ID on a new device.
  • To cancel or change a plan: iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • If a charge looks wrong, Apple handles billing. Start with Apple billing support.

Common issues

  • Paywall after the free round: that is expected. Restore Purchases if you already subscribed.
  • Products not loading: check the internet connection and that the App Store country matches your Apple ID.
  • Onboarding again: deleting the App resets local progress, including free-round flags.
