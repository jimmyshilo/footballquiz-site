The Real Football Quiz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This policy describes how The Real Football Quiz (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by an independent developer. Contact: jimmyshilo@gmail.com.

What the App does not collect

The App does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not currently use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not sell personal data.

Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device (for example via Apple’s UserDefaults), including:

  • whether you have finished onboarding;
  • whether you have used the free solo round and the free team round.

This data stays on the device unless you delete the App or reset it.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment, billing, and subscription status. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Current auto-renewable subscription options:

  • Monthly — displayed in the App at the App Store price for your storefront (reference price USD 2.99);
  • Yearly — displayed in the App at the App Store price for your storefront (reference price USD 24.99).

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Photos and third-party content

Quiz images are bundled in the App. Many photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and similar public sources under their stated licences (including Creative Commons). Source, author, and licence information is kept with the App’s attribution records. Those platforms have their own privacy practices.

Children

The App is a general-audience trivia game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete the App to remove locally stored preferences.
  • Restore or manage purchases through Apple.
  • Email us to ask questions about this policy.

Changes

We may update this page. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
